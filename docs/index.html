Welcome!

The goal of this project is to provide some insight into where lore can be discovered within the game of Elden Ring.
This will include places and items such as:

  • Divine Towers
  • Remembrances
  • Great Runes
  • NPCs (in their first location)(non-player character)
  • Locations important to story progression
  • Bosses important to story progression

So far this site looks at NPC, Divine Tower, and Remembrance first locations and provides a bit of information about them (pulled from the FextraLife Elden Ring wiki) and some random dialogue from the NPCs on the map.
